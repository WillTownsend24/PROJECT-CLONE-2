Overall the project was a success as all terminal tests were completed, satisfying all job stories. How well did we satisfy our stakeholders demands?:

Doctor Robert Wilson wished for a straightforward and non-invasive way to check on his patients dietary habits as well as to contact them professionally. This webApp provides such features as a professional can look at a clients food throughout the day without needing to talk to them personally and pry them for information. The guidance feature is also non-invasive as a client can see such information whenever they wish by choosing to select 'Notifications' in the top right corner of their webpage. Doctor Robert Wilson should therefore be very satisfied with the WebApp we produced sice it meets all his demands.

Noah Wright wanted a system that gives easy recipes based on ingredients so he can reuse ingredients he already had, as well as getting an estimate of cost for meals so that he can budget. Our webApp provides both these features as well as helping to keep track of diets which is something Noah requested. Noah also requested a feature that allows users to filter by healthiness, however a meal cannot be objectively healthy on its own (especially not in a quantifiable manner), healthiness needs to take what is being eaten over the entire day into consideration. To compromise we added a feature that allows you to see how much protein( as Noah requested), fats and carbohydrates are in a meal, which can help give a btter idea of how healthy a users diet is. I believe Noah Wright should be satisfied with the WebApp we produced.

Lydia Cohen wished for an application that allows her to track her meals and assist in healthy eating in order to avoid getting sick. The webApp provides a food logging feature that allows users to get a better idea of what they are eating in terms of calories and nutrients, as well as promoting home cooking by providing recipes, so Lydia Cohen should find it easier to take care of her health by using this app. Lydia Cohen also has dyslexia, so we have provided a visual aid for calorie counting in the form of a bar chart wich should help her. We also added a dyslexia friendly font to make the program more accessible to her. I believe Lydia Cohen should be satisfied with our product.

Clyde Camfield wished for an application which allowed for flexibility in his busy schedule, so that he could message his doctor whenever he had free time and so that he could log food at the end of a shift. Sadly we decided to remove the feature where clients can contact professionals as it seemed unrealistic. Doctors are already overloaded within the NHS and it can take weeks to get an appointment. Taking this into consideration, we should make the work that doctors have to do minimal as its unlikely they would prioritise responding to clients on our app over responding to actual patients on the NHS. It would be unfair to give clients a feature to contact professionals if they are unlikely to get timely responses if any at all, so it would be best to remove the feature altogether.
Clyde was also concerned about how he doesn't take note of hat he eats in the moment and doesn't know how healthy he is eating in the long term, even personally requesting charts. We have provided charts on the client home page so Clyde should be somewhat happy with that.
I believe Clyde may not be completely happy as although we satisfied his wishes for flexibly logging food and data visualisation, we cannot help him contact a doctor for issues more easily, however this is an issue that I believe is outside the scope of this project as the UK helathcare system is notoriously overloaded.

Overall I would say we did a solid job of satisfying our stakeholders' demands however it wan't perfect as some of their demands were not- as far as we were aware- possible for this project.

How did our design change throughout the project?
We slimmed down the design quite alot in terms of making User Interfaces less cluttered and making the webApp more streamlined. We removed raising issues due to its lack of necessacity and we made comunications with professionals one way so that they would not be overwhelmed. We also removed consideration of allergens as the API we used often gave recipes in other languages, and we didn't think the ingredients searching was solid enough to be perfect. We would rather our users be skeptical as to whether a food has allergens so that they do not assume a food they are allergic to is safe.

Conclusion:
In conclusion we created a webApp successfully that meets what the project owner envisaged. Our webApp supports the monitoring of diets, provides a way to give dietary information in the form of guidance from professionals, supports home cooking by providing full recipes and instructions on how to prepare recipes; allows users to search for recipes based on ingredients, allows subscribers to record which recipes they've tried and which are their favourites, allows subscribers to rate and comment on a recipe; allows subscribers to record details of food intake in a 'food diary' and look back over this, allow health professionals to observe their clients and allow health professionals to offer advice to clients. Our project meets all of these standards that the product owner envisions with the only features we didn't include being automatic feedback on diets due to health and safety concerns over a potentially faulty system giving harmful advice.
