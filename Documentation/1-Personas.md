Dr Robert Wilson:

![Dr](<Wireframes/image.png>)

Age: 38 

Role: General Practitioner and Dietician 

Goal: 

“I would like an easy way to stay up to date with my patient’s dietary habits. There are not many good options available other than appointments, and I am aware that many do not like the inconvenience and pressure of them. I think I would be able to do my job much more effectively if there was a system that allowed me to remotely keep an eye on my patient’s health in a non-invasive manner as well as contact them professionally.” 

Frustrations: 

“Some patients, especially ones with underlying health conditions and poor impulse control, tend to need more attention than others. It would be inappropriate to give out my personal phone number to patients, so in the absence of a quick efficient way to contact them, many patients can fly under the radar, making it difficult to help them if their health deteriorates or spot early warning signs for bigger problems. A person with an eating disorder may be afraid to make appointments due to issues with insecurity, and a person with diabetes may eat food inappropriate for them, but I would only find out at our next appointment, which could take a month or longer.” 

Technical ability: 

“I have a moderate level of ability with using a computer; however, I am no expert. The IT guy usually helps me out if anything goes wrong.” 

Motivation: 

“I simply want a straightforward non-invasive way to keep up with my patient’s health dietary habits."

Clyde Camfield 

![Engineer](<Wireframes/image-3.png>)

Age: 52 

Role: Industrial Engineer 

 

Goal: 

"I'm getting on in my years now, and so keeping on top of my health is starting to feel more important than it used to. I'm usually working nights, so it's been kind of hard to focus on making proper meals and keeping up to date with my doctor with my health and such. I would like to be able to plan out my meals nice and easily and message my doctor whenever I have free time in my busy schedule."

"Because of how hectic my schedule can be, I often don't get time to take note of what I'm eating in the moment, so I don’t really have an idea of how I’m eating in the long term. Because of this, I'd love a website to allow me to retroactively note down what I've eaten earlier, and when. It would also be nice if I could get some graphs and charts to compare how I’m eating to what a healthy diet should look like." 

 

Tech Skill: 

 "I don't use the internet all that much, but if a website is intuitive and presented well then I should be able to get the hang of it." 

 

Motivation:
