Product Leader Meeting (In Person)

          Date: 11/03/26
     Attendees: All + Product Leader
     Discussed: In-depth specifics of project requirements
 Retrospective: Good communication, established our goals, took too long to organise due to miscommunication.
         Notes: - Different interfaces -> log in as regular user or doctor
		- API to turn food info to nutrition info
		- Way for subscribers to access their previous logs
		- Compare with nutrition guidelines from GP
		- Diet based on user's attributes such as height/weight
		- Plot a graph to compare
		- System needs to offer clear feedback (recommendation system) — recommends certain meals
		- Users should be able to note favourite dishes + ones they want to try
		- Leave comments under dish
		- Use third parties APIs as much as possible
		(Health professional (hp) side)
		- Needs direct access to subscriber info
		- Subscriber notes starting goal that the HP can see
		- Functionality to assign users to HP
		- Users can pick HP from drop-down menu based on speciality (doctor bio?)
		- Graph and statistics of user food per day
		- Needs way to give advice to clients (notification system) (similar to email?)
		- Database for ingredients
		- Health adviser can also recommend food/ingredients
